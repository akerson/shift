How to play

  • Build mode: tap an empty slot to place a machine. Sources and the goal are fixed.
  • Program mode: tap a slot to turn the arm there (it adds the ↺/↻ steps for you), then add Grab or Drop. Tap a step to insert after it. Each step takes one cycle, and the program loops forever.
  • Grab takes a fresh item from a source, or a finished item from a machine. If the machine is still working, the arm waits. Grabbing when there's nothing to take, or dropping with an empty hand, does nothing. That's how a loop can prime machines on its first pass.
  • Drop puts the held item into a machine. Two-input machines fill both slots, then start on their own.
  • Machine times: washer 2 · cutter, extender, mixer 3 · painter, filler 4 cycles. A machine won't start again until its output is taken.
  • Pass by delivering all the goal shapes. Then try for fewer steps or fewer cycles.
