Shift

Shape & colour prototype. Throwaway, for judging readability.

Palette

These are painter's primaries, so mixing is intuitive: red + yellow = orange, yellow + blue = green, blue + red = purple. Mixing a colour with itself changes nothing. There's also a neutral grey for unpainted shapes, and "hollow" for no fill.

The property space

Rows vary the side count; columns vary the border and fill. Hollow shapes show the floor through them.

At game scale

These are items moving on belts at real tile sizes (24, 32 and 40 px). Tell me which size still reads well. Paint is drawn as a droplet so it's never mistaken for a shape.

Machines: before → after

Each machine does one visible thing. The order of operations matters, and there's often more than one route to a target.

Two routes to the same target

Target: a purple-bordered hexagon filled yellow. Route A mixes the paint first. Route B paints two shapes and blends them. They differ in cost, space and time, and that choice is the puzzle.

Nesting (later): readability check

One level only. The inner shape is drawn at about 45% size. Machines act on the outer layer, and the Washer clears the inside. These are shown at 24, 32, 40 and 64 px.
